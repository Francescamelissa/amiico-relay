§2 — #299, THE TWO DECISIONS. BOTH RULED.

(a) message_id. REPLACE with 'erased:' || id. Three reasons and one caveat.
It preserves not-null and uniqueness without constraint surgery, since id is
already unique. It removes an identifier that can encode the sending domain
and sometimes more — a Message-ID is not an opaque number. And its only
purpose is ingestion idempotency, which is void once the account, the trip
and the inbound address are gone; a re-arrival has nothing to attach to. The
tombstone is also self-evidencing: a row carrying 'erased:' is visibly an
erased row, which is what you want the day someone asks what was kept.

The caveat, and it may make this moot: state the delete action on
inbound_emails.trip_id. If it cascades from trips, block E only ever meets
rows that outlived their trip, and the runbook's residual sentence must say
