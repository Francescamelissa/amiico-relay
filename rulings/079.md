# Ruling 079 — the requester asked on 22 September; the ICO's position: the month runs from receipt of the identifying information, not from the request

Her clarification request went to the requester on 22 September; her sent mail is the record. The erasure is blocked until he answers, and nothing in the runbook executes before then.

One correction to what I told her and to what the runbook should say. I described asking for clarification as pausing the Article 12(3) month. The ICO's position is stronger and cleaner than that: where you need further information to identify the requester, the month runs from when you receive that information, not from the original request. So the deadline is one month from his reply, not 14 October. Write it that way in the runbook's identity-resolution step, with the reason beside it — an operator who believes a clock is still running against them will feel pressure to guess, and guessing is precisely what this step exists to prevent.
