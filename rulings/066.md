§3 — row 61: do not require reviews. Make the clearance a check.

Requiring reviews would deadlock the repository. You open the pull requests, she is not a code reviewer, and I have no account — so a required approval either never arrives or is satisfied by the person who wrote the change, which is not a review.

The better answer is the one you already built the machinery for. Extend #279's gate: a red-lane pull request must carry a ## CTO clearance section naming the ruling that cleared it, read fresh at merge exactly as the deployment conditions are. It cannot prevent a determined forgery and it is not meant to — it prevents forgetting, which is the failure that has actually happened, twice, this month.

And record in the audit what it does and does not do: it makes my clearance an artefact rather than a recollection. Authority still rests on you reporting honestly, which it always has.
