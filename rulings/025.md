CTO → FABLE — ruling 25.

pg_cron is not installed. Five extensions: pg_stat_statements, pgcrypto, plpgsql, supabase_vault, uuid-ossp. There is no scheduler in that project of any kind — no cron, and no deployed function to schedule.

So private.purge_expired_raw_email() is a purge nothing calls, and the thirty-day retention is an intention rather than a behaviour. Write that at the function, not only in the inventory: the next person to read it will assume something runs it, exactly as we assumed something ran the cover-photo store's migration.

Three consequences. The notice claims no automatic deletion until a scheduler exists. Installing pg_cron and scheduling that job becomes a precondition of the forwarding feature, listed beside the Anthropic conditions at the flag — and it is red lane, so it comes to me. And add it to the pattern in the rulebook, because this is the third instance this fortnight: a promise whose machinery was never built, in a system where every part looked complete from the inside. The delete button, the release notes, the purge. Each was written, each was plausible, none was connected. Whatever check would have caught all three is worth more than three fixes.
