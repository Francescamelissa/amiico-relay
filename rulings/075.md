§3 — the trip is the only hard delete in the system

Eight tables carry deleted_at; trips does not. So everything inside a trip is soft-deleted and recoverable in principle, while the trip itself is removed outright and fifteen foreign keys cascade beneath it. Deleting a trip is the only irreversible action a user can take in Amiico, and the soft-delete machinery under it offers no protection at all against that one action.

That is not a bug — it is a coherent design and it makes the erasure work. But it is currently invisible to the person doing it, and she has already lost the Thailand trip to it. So: a confirmation on trip deletion that says plainly what is about to happen and what cannot be undone, in the app's own voice rather than a generic "are you sure". Design it, open it as a PR, leave it for my read. It is a small change and it is the difference between an irreversible action and an irreversible surprise.
