§1 — #286, conversion: cleared on one condition, and it is a money condition

The design is right — rate as integer micro-units with its date, BigInt product, one rounding, formatMinor in and out, nothing stored as money, nothing summed, every figure printing its own rate and date. That is the shape I ruled and you built it.

The condition is this. The Budget page shows a converted total under the ring and converted expense rows. Each is rounded independently, so the sum of the converted rows will not equal the converted total — visibly, on the same screen, in pence. The ≈ carries it honestly and I am not asking you to remove either figure. I am asking you to make the behaviour deliberate rather than incidental: a test that pins a case where converted(Σ) ≠ Σ converted(…), with the comment saying this is intended and why, so that the day someone "fixes" it by accumulating converted values the test explains what they are about to break. And confirm, from the tree rather than from the design, that nothing anywhere sums or stores a converted figure.

Two smaller things into the design's status paragraph: the rate lives on the device only and goes with the phone, like everything else inside a trip; and a rate has a date but no staleness — a September rate shown in December is honest about when it was entered and silent about how old that makes it. Both are acceptable for version one and neither should be discovered later.

The orphaned hub is a real find. app/budget/index.tsx being unreachable means a screen exists that nobody can open, and dead screens are eventually shipped by someone who assumes they work. Queue row of its own with your recommendation — not folded into this PR.

§2 — #280, activity keying: cleared on two conditions, both from last week

The lock and its red are what I wanted, and deleteTrip purging unconditionally rather than only-if-current is the correct reading of the old bug.

First condition: a test that deleting trip A leaves trip B's entries untouched. The seven cover the read lock — two trips never share a list, a write for B leaves A's identity unchanged. None of them covers the delete path, and the delete path is where the cityLegs near-miss lived. That is the assertion I would have wanted on 9 September.

Second, and this is the one I would most expect to be missed: does the activity entry's persisted shape change? It gains tripId. Her phone holds activity entries written by build 18 without one. #276 established exactly what that costs and exactly how to fix it — a version bump with a read-time migration, tested against a payload written by the old build's own code. If the activity store's shape moves, it needs the same treatment and the same kind of test. If it does not move, say so explicitly and say why.

§3 — row 68: it is the interpretation parent again, and the fix is mechanical

A # at the start of a commit-body line is a comment under git's default cleanup. Content placed in a medium that interprets it — the sixth or seventh instance, and the first where the medium was git itself.

File it there rather than as a new clause. And make the fix a function rather than a rule: --cleanup=whitespace on every commit this project makes, so a line beginning # survives. "Never start a line with #" is a discipline, and we have established at some cost what disciplines are worth. Leaving f6c712f as it stands and carrying the sentence in #288's body was right.
