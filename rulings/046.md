§2 — two defects in the document

The city rows render as Mexico City, Mexico City and Oaxaca, Oaxaca — one value printed twice where a second field should be. Find whether the second slot is region, country or admin area, and whether it is being populated with the city name at write time or merely rendered from the wrong field. Those have different fixes and only one of them is cosmetic.

And the date range prints as Mon 28 Dec – Fri 8 Jan with no year, on a range that crosses a year boundary. This is an export — a document someone saves, prints, or hands to a person meeting them at an airport. A date that cannot be resolved without knowing when it was generated is not a date. Same for the per-city dates. The "Generated on" line carries a year; the trip itself does not, which is the wrong way round.

§3 — the fourth residual is account-level, and it travels

Confirmed on a trip created minutes earlier: the emergency contact appears in every trip's export because it hangs off the account rather than the trip. Two consequences beyond the erasure question.

It goes in every export, so the data-portability answer is that a user's export contains a third party's name and mobile number by construction — the notice has to be accurate about that.

And the trip summary is the artefact users are told to save and share. Anyone who sends their summary to a travel companion discloses their emergency contact's phone number, without being told they are doing it. That is not a defect today and I am not asking for a change tonight — but it is a decision that should be made deliberately and written down, rather than discovered by whoever shares one first.
